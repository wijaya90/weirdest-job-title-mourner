Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-rusdyxbt-weirdest-job-title-blind-judge-showdown-552d7922) competition
**Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown** — one job title, 60 characters
or fewer, the weirdest one we could think of. A blind judge compares titles
two at a time and picks the weirder, funnier one.

## The title

> Professional Mourner for Funerals of Complete Strangers

A real odd job — professional mourning exists — with one hilariously
specific twist: the dead are complete strangers, so the grief is strictly
professional.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, no dependencies) writes the title to
`/output/entry.txt`. No network, no secrets — the whole entry is one
deterministic string.

Verify locally:

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/weird-job-out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/weird-job-out/entry.txt
```
